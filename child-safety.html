Fedoly Child Safety Standards

Last Updated: July 30, 2026

Fedoly is committed to providing a safe and respectful environment for all users. We have zero tolerance for Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM). Any content or behavior involving the exploitation or abuse of children is strictly prohibited.

1. Prohibited Content

Users may not:

  • Create, upload, share, request, or distribute Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).
  • Exploit, groom, threaten, or sexually abuse minors.
  • Use the platform to contact minors for sexual purposes.
  • Encourage or facilitate child exploitation in any form.

2. Reporting Abuse

Users can report inappropriate content, messages, or user accounts directly within the application using the available reporting tools. Reports are reviewed as quickly as possible by our moderation team.

3. Enforcement

If we become aware of content or behavior that violates these standards, Fedoly may:

  • Remove the violating content immediately.
  • Suspend or permanently terminate the offending account.
  • Preserve evidence when required by law.
  • Cooperate with law enforcement and relevant authorities where applicable.

4. Compliance

Fedoly complies with applicable child safety laws and regulations. We are committed to taking appropriate action against any confirmed reports of child exploitation.

6. Commitment

Protecting children is one of our highest priorities. We continuously improve our moderation processes and cooperate with relevant organizations and authorities to help prevent child exploitation.
